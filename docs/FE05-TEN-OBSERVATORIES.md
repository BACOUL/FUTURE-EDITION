# FE-05 — Ten Observatories

FE-05 materializes one observatory for each canonical Future Edition question.

## Baseline rule

Each observatory starts with at least five historical events backed by a canonical source, a claim, an evidence record and provenance.

The baseline is intentionally conservative:

- peer-reviewed papers, regulators and official public records are preferred;
- preprints and institutional releases are retained only with lower confidence;
- every baseline claim remains `machine_proposed`;
- every baseline event remains `human_review: required`;
- every event is `change_type: unassessed` until FE-04's Change Engine receives an explicit human-approved assessment transition;
- an event may point at a milestone as a candidate relevance link, but this never means that the milestone is met.

This separation prevents historical curation from silently becoming a scientific-state judgment.

## Gate

FE-05 passes only if all ten canonical questions have an active observatory and at least five verified, fully traceable historical events. Every event must resolve to its question, technology, milestone, claim, evidence, source and provenance without an orphaned relation.
