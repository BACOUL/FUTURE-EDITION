Chronologie fondatrice

5 événements vérifiés.

Chaque entrée ci-dessous remonte à une source canonique et conserve son niveau de confiance.

24 mai 2023
Brain–spine interface

Brain–spine interface enables natural control of standing and walking

A 2023 brain–spine interface study reported natural control over standing and walking in a participant with chronic tetraplegia, with stable use over one year.

Publication · niveau A
7 févr. 2022
Activity-dependent spinal neuromodulation

Spinal neuromodulation restores motor functions in people with complete paralysis

A 2022 study reported rapid restoration of several trunk and leg motor functions with targeted spinal-cord neuromodulation in participants with complete sensorimotor paralysis.

Publication · niveau A
3 oct. 2019
Brain-controlled four-limb exoskeleton

Tetraplegic participant controls four-limb exoskeleton with implanted brain signals

A 2019 report described a tetraplegic participant controlling a four-limb exoskeleton using an implanted brain-machine interface during a 24-month study.

Publication · niveau A
31 oct. 2018
Spatiotemporal epidural stimulation

Targeted neurostimulation supports walking after chronic spinal cord injury

A 2018 study reported that targeted spatiotemporal spinal stimulation enabled voluntary control of walking-related movements in participants with chronic spinal cord injury.

Publication · niveau A
4 juin 2011
Epidural spinal cord stimulation

Epidural stimulation enables standing and voluntary movement after motor-complete paraplegia

A 2011 case report found that lumbosacral epidural stimulation enabled weight-bearing standing and some intentional lower-limb movement in a person with motor-complete paraplegia.

Publication · niveau A
