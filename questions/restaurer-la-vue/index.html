24 mai 2021
Optogenetic vision restoration

Partial visual recovery reported after optogenetic therapy

A 2021 case report described partial functional visual recovery in a blind patient after optogenetic therapy combined with engineered goggles.

Publication · niveau A
19 déc. 2017
RPE65 retinal gene therapy

FDA original approval of Luxturna

FDA records December 19, 2017 as the original approval date for Luxturna for an inherited retinal-dystrophy indication involving biallelic RPE65 mutations.

Régulateur · niveau A
14 juil. 2017
RPE65 retinal gene therapy

Randomized phase 3 trial of voretigene neparvovec

A randomized phase 3 trial reported improved functional vision on the multi-luminance mobility test after voretigene neparvovec for biallelic RPE65-mediated retinal dystrophy.

Publication · niveau A
15 oct. 2014
hESC-derived retinal pigment epithelium transplantation

Longer-term follow-up of hESC-derived retinal pigment epithelium transplantation

An open-label follow-up of 18 treated eyes reported medium-to-long-term safety findings and visual-acuity improvement in 10 eyes after hESC-derived retinal pigment epithelium transplantation.

Publication · niveau A
13 févr. 2013
Retinal prosthesis

FDA humanitarian approval of the Argus II retinal prosthesis

FDA's humanitarian-device database records a February 13, 2013 approval decision for the Argus II retinal prosthesis system.
